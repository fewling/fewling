# Deterministic Bloom

*An algorithmic philosophy for the empty right half of the `fewling` header.*

---

## The movement

**Deterministic Bloom** is the aesthetics of a single source becoming many forms without ever drifting. One origin is planted at the edge of the wordmark, and from it a structure unfolds rightward — generation after generation of nodes, each derived strictly from its parent, each connected by a thread of evidence back to where it began. Nothing in the bloom is decorative noise. Every node is *generated*, in the literal sense: the same seed always grows the same lattice, down to the last pixel. The piece is a portrait of its author's discipline — **contract-first, spec-driven, polyglot, drift-free** — rendered not as a slogan but as a law the algorithm physically cannot break.

This is the quiet conceptual DNA: in generative art the seed is sacred, because a seed must reproduce its output exactly or the art is worthless. In `fewling`'s engineering, the OpenAPI contract is sacred for the same reason — one schema generates the Dart client, the TypeScript functions, the Kotlin DTOs, and *a polyglot system can't drift*. The art and the work share one invariant. Someone who builds contract-first systems will feel it immediately; everyone else simply sees a poised, breathing constellation. That is the point — the reference enhances depth without announcing itself.

## How it manifests algorithmically

The bloom grows by deterministic propagation, not by scattering. A root node sits where the cursor leaves off. Each generation spawns a controlled number of children, their positions a marriage of structure and organic perturbation: the golden angle governs how branches fan, while a single layer of low-frequency noise — sampled from the same seed — bends each child just enough to feel grown rather than drawn. A light relaxation pass then nudges siblings apart so no two nodes collide, the way a crystal settles into the lowest-energy arrangement it can find. Order, but never rigidity. The result must read as **inevitable** — as though, given this seed, the lattice could not have grown any other way.

Edges carry the meaning. Every node remembers its parent, and the faint tracery between them is the *lineage* — the visible proof that each downstream form was generated from the source, never authored independently. Terminal nodes, the leaves of the final generation, are the polyglot clients: the only nodes allowed to glow, small bright confirmations at the far edge that the contract reached them intact. Brightness decays with distance from the origin so the eye is led, gently, from wordmark to bloom and out to the synchronized edge — a visual hierarchy held even inside randomness.

## Temporal behavior

The bloom is mostly still, because restraint is the brief — this header already wore a waveform and shed it for being monotonous, and a master knows when *less motion* is the harder, better choice. So time enters only as a single slow pulse of propagation: a low-amplitude wave of brightness that travels outward from the origin along the lineage, the way a code generator sweeps a schema and emits each client in turn. The pulse never strobes, never loops conspicuously; it is closer to breathing than to animation. At zero speed the piece is fully static and loses nothing — every parameter was tuned so the frozen frame is already a finished composition.

## The standard

This algorithm must feel like the product of deep computational craftsmanship — countless iterations of tuning the branch ratios, the noise amplitude, the relaxation strength, the brightness falloff, until the bloom sits in the negative space as if the header were always designed around it. Not generative noise dressed up as art, but **controlled emergence**: master-level restraint where every threshold was chosen, every palette value (the contract greens `#1D9E75` and `#5DCAA5` against the `#0d1117` card) earns its place, and the whole thing whispers rather than shouts. Understated standout — 低調的突出. The mark of someone at the top of their field leaving a signature so quiet that only the people who'd recognize it ever will.

---

### Parameters this philosophy exposes
- **seed** — the contract; reproduces the exact bloom every time.
- **generations** — how many derivation layers propagate from the source.
- **branching** — children spawned per node (density of the lattice).
- **spread** — vertical fan of each generation (tight column vs. wide canopy).
- **lineage opacity** — subtlety of the connecting threads.
- **leaf glow** — brightness of the terminal "generated clients."
- **pulse speed** — propagation animation; `0` freezes a finished frame.
